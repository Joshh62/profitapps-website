Privacy Policy

Profit Apps Limited · Version 1.2 · Updated 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Profit Apps Limited collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information across all our products and services. This policy explains our approach to personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA 2023).

This policy covers:

● AjoLedger — ajoledger.ng ● ProfitTrack POS — profittrack.ng ● profitapps.ng

And any future products published under the Profit Apps Limited brand.

1. Data Controller

Legal entity: Profit Apps Limited

Registration: Incorporated in Nigeria

Address: Kaduna, Nigeria

Websites: profitapps.ng · ajoledger.ng · profittrack.ng

Contact: hello@profitapps.ng · support@ajoledger.ng

2. Information We Collect

You provide directly:

  • Full name and phone number (required for AjoLedger)
  • Password (stored as bcrypt hash — never in plain text)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • National Identification Number — only when you voluntarily verify identity on AjoLedger
  • Business name and details (ProfitTrack)
  • City, state, biography (optional)

Generated through use:

  • Savings circle details, contribution records, payment history (AjoLedger)
  • Sales records, inventory data, staff activity (ProfitTrack)
  • Messages within your circles
  • Reputation score from payment behaviour (AjoLedger)
We do NOT collect: BVN, bank account numbers, card numbers, facial images, biometric data, or selfie photographs. We never hold group savings funds.

3. NIN Verification (AjoLedger — Optional)

NIN verification on AjoLedger is entirely voluntary. When you choose to verify:

  • You submit your 11-digit NIN
  • We send it to Dojah (dojah.io) via encrypted API for a name-to-NIN lookup against NIMC
  • We store only: the result (verified/not), a masked reference, and the name NIMC returned
  • Your NIN is encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • No photographs, selfies, or biometric data are involved
  • Every admin access to your NIN record is permanently logged

Unlocks: NIN-verified badge, 5% fee discount, access to verified-only circles

4. How We Share Your Data

Within your circles (AjoLedger):
Other circle members can see your name, photo, contribution status, reputation score, and messages. They cannot see your phone number, email, or NIN.

Service providers:

  • Dojah — NIN name lookup (only when you verify on AjoLedger)
  • Twilio — WhatsApp/SMS reminders
  • Paystack — Payment processing (PCI DSS certified)
  • Cloudinary — Photo and file storage
  • Neon / Render — Database and backend hosting
We NEVER sell your data, share with advertisers, or allow third parties to use it for their own purposes.

5. Your Rights (NDPA 2023)

  • Access — request a copy of all your data within 30 days
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate information
  • Erasure — request account and data deletion within 30 days
  • Portability — receive your data in JSON or CSV format
  • Object — opt out of marketing and profiling at any time
  • Withdraw consent — for NIN verification or marketing, at any time

Contact: support@ajoledger.ng or hello@profitapps.ng

NDPC complaints: www.ndpc.gov.ng

6. Data Security

  • All data in transit: TLS 1.2+
  • Passwords: bcrypt hash
  • NIN: AES-256 encryption at rest
  • No biometric data collected or stored
  • Immutable audit log — all actions permanently recorded
  • Superadmin access to identity data requires mandatory logged reason

Data breach notification: NDPC within 72 hours, affected users without undue delay (NDPA 2023, Section 40).

7. Children's Privacy

All Profit Apps products are for adults aged 18 and above only.

  • Users must confirm their age at registration — this is a legal declaration
  • Accounts discovered to belong to minors will be immediately suspended and deleted
  • Parents/guardians should report suspected underage accounts to support@ajoledger.ng
